# Omasheet 0.1.8

Numbers now carry units. A cell can hold `5kg`, `9.81m.s^-2` or `45°30′`, and sums, products, comparisons and conversions follow the units, with an error for a mismatch. Numbers can also carry an uncertainty (`10.5(3)e-3`), `Complex` gets a full set of functions, and the way numbers are shown has changed: three places by default, with a `·` wherever digits are not shown.

## Install or update

On Omarchy and Arch Linux, x86_64 or aarch64:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/librasteve/omasheet/main/install.sh | bash
```

If pacman cannot satisfy a dependency on `qt6`, run `sudo pacman -Syu` and then the line above again. Install from source to run on macOS.

## SI units

A unit is written straight after the number, in SI, with no space: `5kg`, `300cm`, `20°C`. A unit of more than one term is joined with `.` and takes `^` and a whole exponent, `kg.m^2.s^-2`. There is no `/` and no space.

- **Arithmetic.** A sum or a comparison converts the right side to the unit of the left (`5kg + 300g` is `5.3kg`). A product or quotient combines the units, and a result with no dimension is a plain number (`6m / 3cm` is `200`). Two quantities of different dimensions are an error that says so.
- **Conversion.** `5kg.to('g')` gives `5000g`. Celsius differences stay Celsius (`20°C - 10°C` is `10°C`).
- **Prefixes and scales.** All the SI prefixes work on every unit. A prefix and a `.` on their own is a scale: `1k.` is 1000 and `5m.` is 0.005, both exact numbers, and `m` alone is still the metre.
- **Uncertainty.** `10.5(3)e-3kg` and `5kg ± 100g` carry their uncertainty through arithmetic.
- **Columns.** A column can have a quantity type, `Weight : Mass`, and a unit and number format in the brackets, `Weight : Mass[g; .1f]`. A cell in another unit of the same kind is converted to the column's unit, a bare number is read in it, and a cell of another dimension is an error. Quantity columns are set right in the grid like any number.
- **Aggregates.** `sum`, `mean`, `min`, `max` and `median` of a quantity column keep the unit.
- **Parquet.** A quantity column is written to Parquet as a struct of value, uncertainty and unit, and its kind is kept so it opens as the same type. In CSV and JSON a quantity is written as text (`5kg`); an XLSX export writes text and says the unit is dropped. Importing a CSV, JSON or XLSX never turns text like `12g` or `10m` into a quantity.
- The units come from `siquant`, a new Rust crate of SI units and prefixes (github.com/librasteve/siquant). It is taken from crates.io (`siquant` 0.1.2).

## Data, angles and time

- **Data.** The bit `b` and the byte `B` take the SI prefixes (`kB` is 1000 B) and the binary ones (`KiB` is 1024 B). A rate needs no unit of its own (`MiB.s^-1`), and `1Ki.` is 1024. `Data`, `DataRate` and `DataDensity` columns can be shown in any of them, `Data[GiB]`, so a drive's capacity over its platter area comes out in `Tib.m^-2`.
- **Angles.** `rad` is the base unit, with `sr`, `lm` and `lx` built on it, and `°` (also `º`), `′` and `″` as units. `45°30′30″` is a literal, and `dms()` writes an angle that way. `sin`, `cos` and `tan` take an angle in any unit (a plain number is radians), and `arcsin`, `arccos` and `arctan` give back a `rad` quantity. See `examples/angles.omx`.
- **Time.** A quantity of time can be added to a time, date or date-time, `17:05:00 + 10s`, as a `Duration` can, and converts to and from a `Duration`. A time of day wraps at midnight, and a date-time follows the clocks of its zone. See `examples/durations.omx`.

## Approximate equality

`=~=` compares numbers, complex numbers and quantities within a tolerance of 1e-15 of the larger, or absolutely when either is zero: `sin(pi()) =~= 0` is true where `==` is false. An uncertainty is ignored. `==` on a `Float` stays exact, and on a `Scientific` it compares within the combined uncertainty.

## Uncertain numbers

A `Scientific` is a number with an uncertainty in brackets, `10.5(3)e-3`, or written with `±`. The uncertainty propagates through arithmetic and the math functions, takes the number formats, has its own entry in the type menu, and is written to Parquet as a struct (an XLSX export drops the uncertainty). `±` binds tighter than any other operator. See `examples/uncertainty.omx`.

## Complex numbers

- `re`, `im`, `conj` and `arg`, with `abs`, `sqrt`, `exp`, `ln`, the logarithms, and the inverse trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, now take a `Complex`. Their results come back complex where they leave the real line (`arcsin(2)` is `1.571+1.317i`).
- A `Complex` converts to a real only when its imaginary part is exactly zero. The old tolerance that allowed `3+1e-16i` to convert is gone.
- A column of complex results is typed as `Complex`. See `examples/complex.omx`.

## How numbers are shown

- A `Decimal`, a `Float`, the parts of a `Complex` and a `Quantity` are shown to three places after the point. Anything cut is marked with `·` (`1/3` is `0.333·`, `sqrt(2)` is `1.414·`, `arcsin(1)` is `1.571rad·`). A number with three places or fewer is shown whole, and one below a thousandth keeps its digits. Pointing at a cell shows the number in full, and `omasheet eval --exact` and files give every digit.
- Before that, a `Float` or the part of a `Complex` is rounded to 15 places so that the noise of a calculation shows as nothing: `cos(90°)` is `0·` and not `6.123e-17`. The 15 is the `float_places` of the display style, which can be turned off in code but has no setting in the app yet.
- A `Scientific` and a quantity with an uncertainty are not cut: the uncertainty sets the digits.
- `omasheet render` (Markdown and `--html`) sets quantity columns right, as the grid does.

## In the app

- **Type and unit pickers.** Set type opens a picker grouped by domain, with a live check and a master caret, and a unit picker for quantity columns that shows the base unit ticked and examples. Both have Apply and Close buttons and clear buttons, and Tab cycles between the panels.
- **Set format** works like the filter box: typed with a legend panel of examples, checked as you type, and shown in red with a short reason when it is wrong.
- **F4** cycles the Set panels and shows help beside F3's.
- **Whole columns carry their type.** Copying or cutting whole columns and pasting them onto whole columns brings the type, unit and format with them, including when inserted with Insert cells before/after. **Paste format** in the column menu pastes only the type, unit and format, with no values, and replaces Paste transposed there.
- Copy values, selectable error messages, a draggable filter popup, a Clear filter button, filter undo at Apply, a summary that honours filters, and a cell editor that spills past the last column.

## Tests

The engine test suite passes in full. The three tests that failed in earlier builds are settled: `how_much_of_the_corpus_lowers` is removed, and `a_comparison_uses_the_values` and `every_math_function_carries_its_slope` were brought up to date with the comparison tolerance and the inverse trigonometric functions that give a `rad` quantity.

## Known issues

- The `dateformats` example differs on a machine whose locale writes `AM` and `PM` where the example expects `a.m.` and `p.m.`.
- The clipboard changes for whole columns were tried out only by building and starting the app.
