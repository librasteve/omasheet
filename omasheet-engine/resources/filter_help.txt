# The legend in the Operators panel, opened from the Set filter… box.
#
# A section starts `== Title | Families`, and says which columns it is shown
# for: Any, Number (numbers, quantities), Text (String, Email, Url, Uuid), Date
# (Date, Datetime), Time (Time, Datetime), Span (Duration). The lines under it are `what you write |
# what it does | an example`. A line starting `: ` is a note, shown across the
# panel and wrapped. In an example, `{v}` and `{w}` are two sample values of the
# column's own kind: 1 and 10, 'a' and 'm', or two dates. Blank lines and lines starting with `#` are ignored. A row
# is two lines in the panel, the symbol and example above and the meaning
# below, so the meaning may be long; keep the symbol and the example short.

== The cell | Any
: `*` stands for the cell tested, and the filter keeps the rows where it is true. It compares values, not what is shown: `* == 3` finds a cell shown as 3.00. An empty cell fails every comparison, so `* != 3` hides it too.

== Compare | Any
== | is equal to | * == {v}
!= | is not equal to | * != {w}
< | is less than, or before | * < {w}
<= | is less than or equal to | * <= {w}
> | is greater than, or after | * > {v}
>= | is greater than or equal to | * >= {v}

== Range | Number Date Time Span
a < * <= b | comparisons chain, for a range | {v} < * <= {w}

== One of | Any
in [ ] | is one of a list | * in [{v}, {w}]
.is_in([ ]) | the same, as a call | *.is_in([{v}, {w}])

== A range of values | Number
in a..b | is in a range of values | * in 1..5

== Text | Text
.starts_with(s) | starts with s, exactly as written | *.starts_with('A')
.starts_with(a..b) | starts with a letter from a to b | *.starts_with('A'..'C')
.ends_with(s) | ends with s | *.ends_with('son')
.contains(s) | has s in it | *.contains('an')
.matches(re) | matches a regular expression | *.matches('^A.*n$')
.to_lowercase() | lower case first, to ignore case | *.to_lowercase().contains('an')
.to_uppercase() | upper case first | *.to_uppercase() == 'UK'
.len_chars() | the number of characters | *.len_chars() > 3

== Empty and repeated | Any
.is_null() | is empty | *.is_null()
.is_duplicated() | is in the column more than once | *.is_duplicated()
.is_first_distinct() | is the first of its value in the column | *.is_first_distinct()

== Empty text | Text
== '' | is empty text | * == ''
!= '' | is not empty | * != ''

== Not a number | Number
.is_nan() | holds NaN, not a number | *.is_nan()
.is_finite() | holds a finite number | *.is_finite()

== Parts of a date | Date
.year .month .day | the parts of a date | *.year == 2025
.weekday | the day of the week, 1 Monday to 7 Sunday | *.weekday >= 6

== Parts of a time | Time
.hour .minute .second | the parts of a time | *.minute == 0

== Join | Any
and | both are true | * > {v} and * != {w}
or | either is true | * < {v} or * > {w}
not | the opposite | not * == {v}
( ) | group, to say which goes first | (* < {v} or * > {w}) and * != {v}
