# The legend in the Formats panel, opened from the Set format… box.
#
# A section starts `== Title | Families`, and says which columns it is shown
# for: Number (Integer, Decimal, Float, Percent, Fraction, Hex, Octal, Binary),
# Scientific, Quantity, Date (Date, Time and Datetime), Boolean. The lines under it are
# `what you write | what it does | an example`, the example being a format and
# then, after `→`, what it shows. A line starting `: ` is a note, shown across
# the panel and wrapped. Blank lines and lines starting with `#` are ignored.
# A row is two lines in the panel, the symbol and example above and the meaning
# below, so the meaning may be long; keep the symbol and the example short.

== Shape of a format | Number Scientific Quantity
: A format is flags, a width, .places and a conversion, in that order. Only the conversion is needed. `08.2f` is zero fill, width 8, 2 places, fixed: 00012.50.

== Flags | Number Scientific Quantity
' | group the digits in threes | '.0f → 1,234
+ | always show the sign | +.1f → +2.5
(space) | a space where a + would go | " .1f" → " 2.5"
0 | fill the width with zeros | 08.2f → 00012.50
- | align left in the width | -8.1f → 2.5

== Width and places | Number Scientific Quantity
8 | at least 8 characters wide | 8.1f → "     2.5"
.2 | 2 places after the point | .2f → 3.14

== Conversions | Number
d | a whole number | d → 42
f | fixed places | .2f → 3.14
e | a mantissa and an exponent | .3e → 3.142e0
g | whichever of f and e reads better | .3g → 3.14
x X | hexadecimal, in lower or upper case | x → ff
o | octal | o → 17
b | binary | b → 101
# | a prefix: 0x, 0o, 0b | #x → 0xff

== Conversions | Scientific Quantity
f | fixed places | .2f → 10.50
e | a mantissa and an exponent | .3e → 1.050e1
e-3 | the same exponent for every cell, here 10^-3 | .1e-3 → 10500.0e-3
g | whichever of f and e reads better | .3g → 10.5

== Uncertainty | Scientific Quantity
(nothing) | the value with its uncertainty in brackets | .2f → 10.50(30)
± | value ± uncertainty | ±.2f → 10.50 ± 0.30
~ | the value alone | ~.2f → 10.50

== Quantities | Quantity
unit | the unit the column shows and holds | Mass[g]
; | then a number format | g; ±.1f → 10.5 ± 0.3g

== Date | Date
%Y %y | year: 2025 or 25 | %Y → 2025
%m %b %B | month: 01, Jan or January | %b → Jan
%d %-d | day: 05, or 5 with no padding | %-d → 5
%a %A | weekday: Fri or Friday | %a → Fri
%j | day of the year | %j → 031

== Time | Date
%H %I | hour: 24 hour or 12 hour | %H → 17
%M %S | minute and second | %M → 05
%f %3f | fraction of a second, or 3 digits of it | %3f → 250
%p | AM or PM | %p → PM
%Z %z | the zone: BST, or +0100 | %Z → BST

== As the locale writes it | Date
%x | a date | %x → 31/01/2025
%X | a time | %X → 17:05:00
%c | a date and a time | %c → 31/01/2025 17:05

== Symbols | Boolean
: A format is any two different characters: the symbol for true and then the one for false. A cell may be written as true or false, as 1 and 0, x and o, or as the column's own two symbols; the format only says how it is shown. Not a space, a ] or a %.
10 | 1 for true, 0 for false | 10 → 1
xo | x for true, o for false | xo → x
XO | X for true, O for false | XO → X
✓✗ | any two characters you like | ✓✗ → ✓
